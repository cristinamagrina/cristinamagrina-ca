2. What data is processed and why

This website has no forms, user accounts, analytics or advertising systems. The only data processed is the following:

  • Technical visit data. When you access the site, the hosting provider unavoidably receives technical connection data (such as your IP address, browser, and date and time of access), which is needed to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. I do not use it to identify you or to build profiles.

Please do not include health data or other especially sensitive information in a first email: standard email is not a fully secure channel. If you become a client, the processing of your clinical data will be governed by the information and consent provided to you before care begins, not by this policy.

3. Legal basis

  • Replying to your messages and arranging an appointment: your consent when you write to me (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) and pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Technical visit data: the legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working properly (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
