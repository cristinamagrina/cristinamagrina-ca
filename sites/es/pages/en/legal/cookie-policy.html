1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can store in your browser or device when you visit it. They are used, for example, to remember your preferences or to measure how the site is used. The applicable rules (Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002, LSSI-CE, and the GDPR) require you to be informed and to give your consent before cookies that are not strictly necessary are used.

2. Cookies used by this website

This website does not use first-party or third-party cookies, nor other storage or tracking technologies on your device (such as browser local storage or tracking pixels). That is why you will not see a cookie notice or banner, and you do not need to accept anything to browse.

  • There are no analytics or advertising tools.
  • There is no embedded third-party content (videos, maps or social networks) that could install cookies.
  • Typefaces are served from this same site, without connecting to third-party services.

3. Links to other websites

This site includes links to third-party services (for example, LinkedIn, Gmail, Outlook or Google Maps). Those services only load if you click the link and may use their own cookies, which are governed by their respective policies.

4. How to manage cookies in your browser

Although this site does not use cookies, you can configure your browser to block or delete cookies in general. You will find instructions in your browser's help: Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.

5. Changes to this policy

If cookies or similar technologies are introduced in future (for example, for analytics), this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before they are used, except for those that are strictly necessary.
