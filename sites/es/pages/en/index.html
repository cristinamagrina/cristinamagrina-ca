Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Send an email to {{email}} to book a free 15-minute consultation, in person or by video call. This time will help me learn more about what brings you to therapy and what you hope to get out of it, and give you a chance to ask any questions you may have. We will also talk about what therapy would look like and next steps to follow.

Do you offer sessions in person or online?

I offer both. In-person sessions take place at my practice at C/ Muntaner, 356 (Sarrià-Sant Gervasi, Barcelona), and online sessions by secure video call, from your home or another private space.

How much do sessions cost?

I'll let you know the different fees when we arrange the first consultation.

Do I need a referral from my physician to get started?

No, a referral is not needed. You can contact me directly if you believe you or your child could benefit from therapy.
