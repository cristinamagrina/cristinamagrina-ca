Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Use the booking button to schedule a free 15-minute consultation. This time will help me learn more about what brings you to therapy and what you hope to get out of it, and give you a chance to ask any questions you may have. We will also talk about what therapy would look like and next steps to follow.

Do you offer sessions in person or online?

I currently offer teletherapy in Canada via secure video call. Clients can be in Ontario, Quebec or Nova Scotia and sessions take place from your home or another private space.

Are services covered by OHIP?

No, psychological services are not covered by OHIP. However, many private insurance plans provide full or partial coverage for psychological services. Please contact your insurance provider to determine your level of coverage.

Can I use my extended health benefits to pay for therapy sessions?

Yes. Most extended health benefits plans cover in part or in full therapy sessions with regulated psychologists or psychological associates registered with the College of Psychologists and Behaviour Analysts of Ontario (CPBAO). Please connect with your insurance provider to determine your level of coverage.

Do I need a referral from my physician to get started?

No, a referral is not needed. You can contact me directly if you believe you or your child could benefit from therapy.
