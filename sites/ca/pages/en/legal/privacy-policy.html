Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The privacy of visitors to this website is important to Cristina Magriñá Psychology. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through this website, how it may be used, and how it is protected.

1. Information Collected Through This Website

This website is hosted and managed through GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub Pages and the technologies used on the website (including Google Fonts, used to display the website's typography) may automatically collect certain technical information as part of their standard hosting and delivery logs, such as your IP address, browser and device information, approximate location, pages visited, referral information, and information about how you use the website.

This website does not itself use cookies, analytics, or marketing tracking technologies. GitHub Pages and Google Fonts may process the technical information described above in accordance with their own privacy policies, independently of this website. The specific technologies used may change as website functionality or third-party services change.

2. Contacting Cristina by Email

The booking and contact buttons on this website do not submit any information to this website, to GitHub Pages, or to any other third party. Clicking one opens an email addressed directly to info@cristinamagrina.ca — using your device's own email app, Gmail, or Outlook, whichever you choose — with a suggested subject line already filled in that you can edit before sending.

Any information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, telephone number, or the content of your message, is sent directly from your own email account to Cristina Magriñá's inbox, the same way as writing her an email yourself. This website does not see, collect, or store that information at any point.

Please do not include sensitive, confidential, or detailed health information in your message. Email is a convenient way to reach out with general questions, but it is not a secure channel for communicating sensitive clinical information.

If you subsequently engage psychological services, information required for those services will be collected and managed through separate systems and procedures as part of the client intake and consent process.

3. How Website Information Is Used

Information collected through this website may be used to:

  • operate, maintain, and secure the website;
  • understand website traffic and usage;
  • improve website content and functionality;
  • evaluate marketing and search-engine optimization; and
  • comply with applicable legal requirements.

Personal information collected through the website will not be sold or rented to third parties.

4. Protecting Your Information

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information collected through the website against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

However, no website, internet transmission, or email communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure. For this reason, please avoid sending sensitive or confidential information by email.

5. Third-Party Services

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts for its typography. These providers may process technical or usage information in connection with providing their services and have their own privacy policies. This website does not otherwise share information with third parties.
