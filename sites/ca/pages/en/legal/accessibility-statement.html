Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026

Cristina Magriñá Psychology is committed to making this website accessible and usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, speech recognition software, or keyboard-only navigation.

What web accessibility means

An accessible website allows people with disabilities to access information and navigate the site with a similar level of ease and independence as other visitors. Accessibility can be supported through both the design and functionality of the website and the use of assistive technologies.

Conformance status

This website has been developed with the aim of meeting the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. This is an accessibility goal rather than a certified audit result. The site has been developed and manually tested with these guidelines in mind, but it has not been independently audited or certified.

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and I will continue to review and improve the website where possible.

Accessibility measures taken on this site

  • The language of the site and each page is declared in the markup.
  • Pages use a clear and logical heading structure, with a reading order that follows the intended visual order.
  • Landmark regions, including the header, navigation, main content, and footer, are identified so they can be navigated using assistive technologies.
  • A “Skip to content” link is the first item reachable by keyboard on every page.
  • All website functionality, including the navigation menu and expandable question panels, can be operated using a keyboard alone.
  • Visible focus indicators are provided for interactive elements.
  • Text and background colour combinations have been selected to meet applicable colour-contrast requirements.
  • Images that convey information include alternative text, while decorative images are hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Motion and animation are reduced automatically for visitors whose system requests reduced motion.
  • Text can be resized and the layout reflows without loss of content or functionality.

Known limitations

While I have taken steps to make this website accessible, some content or functionality may not be fully accessible to everyone. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact me so that I can understand the issue and, where possible, find an alternative way to provide the information or assistance you need.

Requests, issues, and suggestions

If you experience an accessibility issue on this website, require information in an alternative format, or need assistance contacting me to arrange an appointment, you are welcome to get in touch:
